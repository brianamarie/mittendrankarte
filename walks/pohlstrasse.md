# Walking the Pohlstraße

One street, as an example. The walk starts at one end, ends at the other, and takes every pin on the street in the order you would meet it.

The Pohlstraße runs through the middle of the Kiez, from the Gleisdreieck side to the Kluckstraße. On the map it carries 26 pins and 109 articles. A pin with a house number sits at that door. A pin that only says Pohlstraße sits on the roadway, and several articles share it.

## Where it starts

The walk starts at the east end, where the Pohlstraße meets the Flottwellstraße and the Dennewitzstraße. This is the Gleisdreieck side, under the U-Bahn viaduct. The first doors are the low numbers. The first pin is Pohlstraße 4, at 52.50017, 13.37029.

## Where it ends

The walk ends at the west end, where the Pohlstraße meets the Kluckstraße. The articles call this the upper end of the street. The last pin is Pohlstraße 97, at 52.50186, 13.35966.

## How the walk goes

You walk west, a little uphill to the north, about 700 metres. House numbers rise as you go. Four of the 26 pins are the street itself, not a door. Those four sit in the middle of the walk and hold 65 of the articles between them.
